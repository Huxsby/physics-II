## Algorithm & Core Concept

- **FABRIK: A fast, iterative solver for the inverse kinematics problem**  
  Andreas Aristidou & Joan Lasenby (Graphical Models, 2011). This paper introduces the FABRIK algorithm—a heuristic, forward‑and‑backward iterative method that avoids complex angle/matrix math and converges quickly while producing realistic poses.

- **Extending FABRIK with Model Constraints**  
  Andreas Aristidou, Yiorgos Chrysanthou & Joan Lasenby (Computer Animation and Virtual Worlds, first online February 2015). This work enhances FABRIK with support for closed-loop chains, leaf joints, inter-joint constraints, unreachable-target optimization, and convergence proofs.
